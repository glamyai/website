GLAMY PRIVACY NOTICE

Last Updated: 05.19.2026

This Privacy Notice explains how Glamy and Glamy-powered services process personal data in connection with customer communications, operational workflows, AI-assisted interactions, and guest experience services.


By using services powered by Glamy, you acknowledge and understand the practices described in this notice.

DATA CONTROLLER AND PROCESSOR ROLES

In most operational deployments, the business, hotel, clinic, or organization using Glamy-powered services acts as the primary data controller for customer communications and operational data.

Glamy generally operates as a data processor or infrastructure service provider acting on behalf of the applicable business operator.

The applicable business operator is generally responsible for:

- determining the lawful basis for processing
- obtaining legally required notices or consents
- handling customer-facing privacy requests where required
- configuring retention and operational workflows

In limited cases, Glamy may process anonymized or aggregated operational datasets designed to reduce the ability to reasonably identify individuals or specific customers for analytics, security, reliability, quality assurance, or service improvement purposes.

1. WHAT DATA MAY BE PROCESSED

Depending on the services enabled by the operator, hotel, clinic, or business using Glamy, the platform may process:

- text and voice conversations
- operational requests and service interactions
- communication metadata
- device and session information
- customer support interactions
- technical logs and security events

In some cases, limited structured preference data may also be stored to improve continuity and personalization of the service experience.

For hospitality-oriented services, this may include:

- preferred communication language
- number of guests
- room preferences
- dining or dietary preferences
- hospitality-related guest notes
- conversation continuity and consent status

2. PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

Data may be processed for purposes including:

- delivering requested services
- executing operational workflows
- improving communication quality
- customer support and troubleshooting
- fraud prevention and security monitoring
- analytics and operational reporting
- service quality evaluation
- AI system safety and performance improvement

2.1 LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

Depending on the applicable jurisdiction, the nature of the service, and the operational workflow involved, personal data may be processed under one or more lawful bases, including:

- performance of a contract or requested service
- legitimate business or operational interests
- compliance with legal obligations
- customer consent where required by applicable law

Operational activities such as guest communication handling, reservation coordination, workflow execution, and customer support are generally processed as part of delivering requested services.

Activities related to analytics, operational reliability, fraud prevention, abuse monitoring, security, service quality evaluation, and workflow improvement may be processed under legitimate interest grounds where permitted by applicable law.

Where required by law or by the applicable business operator’s policies, explicit consent mechanisms may be used for certain categories of personalization, sensitive data processing, marketing activities, or customer preference storage.

The applicable business operator remains responsible for determining and documenting the appropriate lawful basis for customer-facing processing activities within their jurisdiction or industry.

3. AI-ASSISTED PROCESSING


Glamy-powered services may use automated systems, including AI-assisted technologies, to analyze conversations and support operational workflows.

Glamy is designed around human-configured workflows, operational rules, and business-defined process controls. AI-assisted systems are intended to operate within configured workflow boundaries, operational policies, routing logic, and platform restrictions defined by the applicable business operator.

Glamy is not intended to function as an autonomous agent platform that independently controls operational workflows or makes unrestricted decisions outside configured workflow behavior.

These systems may assist with:

- routing requests
- extracting operational information
- generating suggested responses
- workflow orchestration
- conversation summarization
- personalization and continuity features

Automated systems are intended to operate under human-configured workflows, platform-defined policies, operational rules, workflow controls, authorization boundaries, and security restrictions.

AI-assisted components may support activities such as classification, routing, summarization, response generation, workflow orchestration support, or operational assistance. However, Glamy is not intended to independently make solely automated legally significant decisions about individuals without human-defined workflow governance or business-controlled operational rules.

Limited human access or review may occur in restricted operational scenarios for:

- support
- quality assurance
- abuse prevention
- security investigations
- operational troubleshooting

Such access is intended to be restricted through role-based access controls, operational authorization policies, audit logging, and internal access limitations.

Glamy personnel are not intended to routinely monitor customer conversations or use identifiable customer communications for unrestricted internal review.

Access to identifiable operational data is intended to be limited to authorized operational, security, reliability, or support purposes where reasonably necessary for service delivery, abuse prevention, incident investigation, legal compliance, or platform reliability activities.

4. DATA MINIMIZATION AND PRIVACY PROTECTION

Glamy applies technical and organizational measures intended to reduce unnecessary exposure of personal data.

Depending on the system configuration, these measures may include:

- encryption
- role-based access controls
- audit logging
- retention controls
- automated PII detection and redaction technologies
- masking or redaction systems
- anonymization or pseudonymization techniques
- metadata separation
- restricted operational access

Where technically and operationally possible, Glamy may transform data into anonymized or aggregated datasets designed to reduce the ability to identify individuals.

5. ANONYMIZED AND AGGREGATED DATA

Certain data may be transformed into aggregated or anonymized datasets designed to reduce the ability to reasonably identify an individual.

These datasets may be retained and used for:

- analytics
- operational insights
- quality assurance
- AI-assisted system evaluation
- safety testing
- service improvement
- system reliability monitoring

Where permitted by applicable law, Glamy may use anonymized or aggregated operational datasets for internal analytics, workflow quality improvement, operational reliability analysis, safety testing, and service improvement activities.

These datasets are intended to exclude direct customer identifiers, stable customer identifiers, tenant linkage information, and reversible identity mappings.

6. MEMORY AND PERSONALIZATION FEATURES

Where enabled by the service operator, Glamy may store limited structured preference or continuity information to improve customer experience.

This information is intended to support:

- personalization
- workflow continuity
- operational coordination
- guest experience improvements

Stored preference data is not intended to function as a full conversation archive or permanent behavioral profile.

Where applicable, customers may request deletion of stored preference data in accordance with applicable privacy regulations and operational requirements.

7. SENSITIVE INFORMATION

Glamy is not intended to function as:

- a medical records platform
- a healthcare diagnostic system
- an insurance records platform
- a government identity storage platform


Customers should avoid sharing highly sensitive personal information unless explicitly required for the requested service.

In some operational contexts, certain limited categories of sensitive or preference-related information may be processed where necessary for hospitality, customer experience, accessibility, wellness, or service coordination purposes.

Examples may include:

- dietary or dining preferences
- accessibility or accommodation requests
- hospitality-related comfort preferences
- language or communication preferences

Glamy is not intended to process or retain highly sensitive regulated information unless explicitly required for a supported operational workflow and lawfully handled by the applicable business operator.

Examples of information that should generally not be shared through Glamy-powered services unless specifically required include:

- medical diagnoses or treatment records
- government-issued identifiers
- payment card credentials
- banking passwords or authentication secrets
- insurance records
- highly sensitive financial information

Depending on the jurisdiction and use case, certain categories of information such as health-related details, dietary preferences, religious preferences, wellness-related requests, or accessibility accommodations may be considered sensitive data under applicable privacy laws.

The applicable business operator remains responsible for determining whether additional notices, lawful bases, explicit consent mechanisms, or regulatory obligations apply to the processing of sensitive data within their specific jurisdiction or industry.

7.1 CHILDREN AND MINORS

Glamy-powered services are generally intended for operational customer communication and are not specifically directed toward children.

However, in certain operational contexts such as hospitality, travel, reservations, family services, accessibility coordination, or guest management workflows, limited information relating to minors or family-related requests may be incidentally processed as part of delivering requested services.

Examples may include:

- family reservation details
- guest counts involving children
- child accommodation requests
- accessibility or dietary preferences communicated by parents or guardians

Glamy is not intended to knowingly collect or build behavioral advertising profiles relating to children.

The applicable business operator remains responsible for determining whether additional notices, parental consent requirements, age-related restrictions, or regulatory obligations apply within their jurisdiction, industry, or operational workflows.
